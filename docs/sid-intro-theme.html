What this is. A model of the C64 player planned in music.md, not a generic synth. Three voices, one oscillator each. The player runs on a 50 Hz frame tick and writes a 25-byte shadow of $D400; the audio engine only reacts to those registers, so what you hear is what the SID would be told to do.
Pitches come from the octave-6 note table right-shifted per octave, exactly as the plan specifies — the same truncation the 6510 will produce.
Voice 3 is shared. Kick, snare and hat steal it from the arpeggio for 5, 4 and 2 frames. Turn the steal off to hear the arpeggio uninterrupted.
No filter. The SID filter is chip-dependent and the design avoids it; the only shaping here is a fixed output limiter.
